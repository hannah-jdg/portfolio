Overview

For my practicum placement, the Communications team at TRIUMF assigned me the task to redesign their impact page on the TRIUMF website.

The Problem

The previous impact page was originally just a list of KPI’s and scientific achievements that had been copied in when the entire website was redesigned. However the list did not communicate the full extent of TRIUMF’s impact and all the other areas in which TRIUMF has made a difference.

The Goal

We wanted the page to do a few key things:

Communicate to both funders and the public why they should care about what TRIUMF is doing.

Have the achievements be more accessible and legible for non-scientists to help everyone’s understanding.

Tell a story. Add some colour. Create something new and different for the website.
